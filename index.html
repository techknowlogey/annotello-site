Install

Up and running in a minute

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

  1. Download and open the installer

    Open Annotello-1.2.4.dmg and drag Annotello into your Applications folder.

  2. Open Annotello

    Annotello is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any app. If macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet, click Open.

  3. Grant Screen Recording

    The welcome guide walks you through it. Then press ⌃ ⇧ C and capture something.

  4. Stay up to date automatically

    Annotello checks for new versions once a day and installs them in one click. You can also choose Check for Updates… from the menu bar icon.

FAQ

Questions

Is Annotello free?

Yes. There's no account, subscription or trial.

Does Annotello upload my screenshots?

No. Captures, recordings, text recognition, redaction and captions all happen on your Mac. The only exceptions are features you turn on yourself: AI actions (sent to Anthropic using your own API key) and "Upload & Copy Link" (sent to Imgur).

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Since version 1.2.2, Annotello is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, which scans it for malicious software. Updates are also cryptographically signed, so Annotello only installs updates that come from us.

Does Steps record what I type?

No. Steps captures a screenshot when you click and notes which button, menu or field you clicked. It never records keystrokes or the contents of text fields. With Accessibility permission allowed, captions can name what you clicked; without it they simply say which app you clicked in.

Why does it need Screen Recording permission?

macOS requires it for any app that captures the screen. Microphone, Camera, Accessibility and Speech Recognition are optional and only used for the features that need them.

Where are my captures saved?

In Pictures › Annotello by default. You can change the folder — including to iCloud Drive — in Settings.

How do I update?

From version 1.2.2 on, Annotello updates itself: it checks daily and asks before installing. Choose Check for Updates… from the menu bar icon to check right away, or turn automatic checks off in Settings › General. On 1.2.1 or earlier, download 1.2.2 once and replace the app in Applications. Your captures and settings are kept.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Annotello and drag it from Applications to the Trash. Your captures stay in Pictures › Annotello until you delete them.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes — the download runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel. macOS 15 Sequoia or later is required.
