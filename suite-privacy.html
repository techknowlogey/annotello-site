Annotello Suite Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

This policy covers the Annotello Suite apps for Mac: Annotello Mail (with Calendar and Contacts), Annotello Docs, Annotello Sheets and Annotello Slides. It explains what data the apps use, where it's kept, and who it's shared with.

In short: the apps run on your Mac and keep your data there. There is no Annotello server, account or analytics. The apps talk directly to the services you sign in to, such as Google, and only to provide the features you use.

Data the apps store

Your mail, calendar events, contacts, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, rules and settings are stored on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Annotello Suite. Sign-in tokens and app passwords are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key kept in your macOS Keychain. Annotello, the developer, never receives a copy of any of it.

The apps don't collect analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

Google user data

If you choose Sign in with Google, Annotello Mail asks Google for permission to:

  • Read, send, and organize your Gmail (gmail.modify): to show your mail, send messages you write, and apply changes you make, such as labels, read status, archiving and deleting. Changes are synced back to Gmail so your mail looks the same everywhere.
  • See and edit your Google Calendar (calendar): to show your calendars and save events you create or change.
  • See your Google Contacts (contacts.readonly): to show your contacts and suggest addresses as you type.
  • Your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile): to show which account is signed in.

So that one sign-in also turns on the Gemini assistant, the app asks for one more permission. You can untick it on Google's consent screen and turn Gemini on later:

  • See and manage your Google Cloud projects (cloud-platform): used only to find the Google Cloud project that Google AI Studio made for your Gemini usage, or to create one named “Annotello Gemini” and turn on the Gemini API in it, and then to send your Gemini requests through that project. The app doesn't read or change anything else in your Google Cloud account.

This data goes directly between Google and the app on your Mac. It is stored only on your Mac, as described above, and is used only to provide these features to you.

Annotello doesn't sell Google user data, doesn't use it for advertising, and doesn't use it to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. No person at Annotello reads it.

Annotello Suite's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

If you connect Gmail with an app password instead, the same data is read over Gmail's IMAP and SMTP servers, with the same handling.

Add-ins you choose to connect

Add-ins for Gemini, Claude, Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Slack are off until you sign in to them. Once connected, they receive content only when you use them, or when a mail rule you create tells them to:

  • Gemini (Google): the text you ask it to work on, such as a thread to summarize, a draft to improve or a spreadsheet range to explain. It's sent to Google's Gemini API under your own Google Cloud project or API key, so usage counts toward your own free tier or billing. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use prompts and responses to improve its products, as described in the Gemini API terms. That doesn't apply once billing is turned on for your project.
  • Claude (Anthropic), if you choose it as your assistant instead: the text you ask it to work on, such as a thread to summarize or a draft to improve. It's sent under your own Anthropic account.
  • Zoom and Microsoft Teams: the title and time of a meeting when you add a meeting link.
  • Slack: the message or event you share to a channel.
  • Custom add-ins you install: what you send to them through the add-in's own actions, after you approve its permissions.

Each of these services handles that data under its own privacy policy. Google user data is shared with them only when you direct it, to provide the feature you asked for.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your Mac until you delete it.

  • Signing out of Google in the profile menu removes the synced Gmail, Calendar and Contacts data and the stored sign-in from your Mac.
  • Disconnecting an add-in removes its stored sign-in.
  • Deleting the Annotello Suite folder in your Library removes everything.
  • You can also revoke the apps' access at any time on your Google Account's third-party connections page.

Children

The apps aren't directed at children under 13, and Annotello doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A material change to how Google user data is handled will be announced in the apps before it takes effect.
