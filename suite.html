Questions

Where is my data stored?

On your Mac, in your user Library. The apps talk directly to Google, Zoom, Microsoft and Slack. There's no Annotello server in between.

How are sign-ins protected?

Sign-in tokens and app passwords are encrypted with AES-256, using a key kept in your macOS Keychain. Disconnecting removes them.

Do I need all four apps?

No. Install only the ones you want. They share Drive and hand files to each other when more than one is installed.

How does this relate to Annotello Capture?

They're siblings in the Annotello family. Annotello Capture is the screen capture and annotation app; the Suite apps install separately and don't change it.

Which Macs are supported?

Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later. The apps are signed and notarized by Apple.

What doesn't work yet?

Sending file attachments through Gmail (share a Drive link instead), and Intel Macs.
